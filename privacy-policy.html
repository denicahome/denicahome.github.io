Who operates this service

This notice applies to the Denica Home internal Amazon Ads reporting tool, which is being developed for Denica Home's own advertising account and business operations. The service operator is [full legal business or individual name], located at [business address].

Information accessed from Amazon

If an authorized account holder connects an Amazon Advertising account, the tool may access advertiser account identifiers and advertising data such as campaigns, ad groups, targeting and keyword settings, impressions, clicks, attributed results, and advertising spend. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account holder. The tool is not designed to request or process Amazon retail customer payment details or login credentials.

How information is used

Advertising information is used only to prepare Denica Home's internal sales, advertising performance, and spend reports and to answer questions about those reports. The tool is intended for read-only reporting; it is not used to create, edit, pause, or otherwise change advertising campaigns. The information is not sold or used to build profiles of Amazon shoppers.

Storage and sharing

The planned first version is intended to run on the Denica Home operator's computer. Advertising data and authorization tokens are intended to be stored on that computer, rather than in a separate analytics cloud database. The operator must confirm the final storage, backup, and security arrangements before launch.

The tool will not sell advertising data or send it to outside analytics or AI providers. If the operator enables daily report delivery to a selected WhatsApp or Slack channel, the report's sales, advertising spend, and performance summaries will be sent to that messaging provider and account. The messaging provider will process that message under its own terms and privacy notice. Amazon authorization tokens and full underlying report files are not intended to be included in those messages.
