Privacy

Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Who operates this tool

This notice covers Denica Home's internal Amazon sales and advertising reporting tool. Denica Home is the brand name used by the tool operator. For privacy questions or requests, contact Denica Home through Instagram @homedenica.

Information accessed

When an authorized account holder connects an Amazon account, the tool is designed to retrieve advertiser account identifiers and advertising information such as campaigns, ad groups, targeting and keyword settings, impressions, clicks, attributed sales, and advertising spend. It may also retrieve aggregate seller sales and order performance information needed for daily reporting. The tool does not request Amazon passwords and is not designed to retrieve individual shoppers' payment details or personal profiles.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare Denica Home's internal sales, advertising performance, and spend reports and to answer questions about those reports. The tool is intended for read-only reporting; it is not used to create, edit, pause, or otherwise change advertising campaigns. Data is not sold or used to build profiles of Amazon shoppers.

Storage and sharing

The tool is designed to run on the Denica Home operator's computer. Retrieved reports and Amazon authorization tokens are intended to be stored locally on that computer, not in a separate analytics cloud database. Access to the computer and stored credentials is controlled by its operator.

Advertising and sales data is not sent to outside analytics or AI providers. If daily report delivery is enabled, report summaries containing sales, advertising spend, and performance figures are sent only to the WhatsApp or Slack account selected by the operator. The selected messaging provider processes those messages under its own terms and privacy notice. Amazon authorization tokens and full underlying report files are not included in those messages.

Retention and access revocation

Locally stored reports are kept until the operator deletes them. Amazon authorization tokens are kept only while access is needed and are deleted when the operator disconnects the Amazon account. An account holder can also withdraw the tool's authorization through Amazon account settings. Revoking authorization prevents future API access.

Legal basis and privacy requests

The tool accesses an Amazon account only after its account holder authorizes the connection. Information is processed for the limited purpose of internal business reporting. Where applicable, the tool operator relies on the account holder's authorization and the operator's legitimate interest in reviewing its own sales and advertising results.

You may ask what information is held, request correction or deletion, or raise another privacy concern by contacting Denica Home through Instagram @homedenica. Requests will be handled by the tool operator under the laws that apply.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated if the tool's data sources, uses, storage, or sharing practices change. The effective date above shows when the current version took effect.
