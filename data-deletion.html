Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Revoke Instagram access

Open Instagram settings, find the section for website permissions or apps and websites, select the Denica Home publishing integration, and remove its access. Instagram may change the names or location of these controls.

Request deletion from Denica Home

Send a message to Instagram @homedenica with the subject “Data deletion request.” Include the Instagram username connected to the integration so the account owner can identify the local connection. Do not include a password, one-time code, or access token.

The account owner will remove the saved Instagram account ID, username, access token, and any local publishing records associated with that connection. This does not delete posts already published to Instagram; delete those from Instagram directly.

See the Instagram App Privacy Notice for more information.
