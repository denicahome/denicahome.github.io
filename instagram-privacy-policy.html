Privacy

Instagram App Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Who operates this integration

Denica Home operates an internal tool to connect its professional Instagram account, @homedenica, and publish content selected by the account owner. The integration is not currently connected until the account holder authorizes it.

Information accessed

If you authorize the integration, it may receive the Instagram account ID, username, account type, and an access token from Meta. When the account owner chooses to publish a Reel, the video and caption they provide are sent to Meta for publishing. The integration does not request or access Instagram direct messages, comments, advertising information, or audience analytics.

How information is used

Account information and the access token are used only to connect to @homedenica and carry out content publishing requested by the account owner. The tool does not sell this information or use it to profile Instagram users.

Storage and sharing

The account ID, username, and access token are stored in a restricted local configuration file on the account owner's computer. The video and caption are sent to Meta when the owner initiates publishing. Meta processes the API request and content under its own terms and privacy policy. The tool does not send Instagram account information to advertising or analytics providers.

Retention and revocation

The account owner may disconnect the integration by revoking its access in Instagram settings and removing the saved connection details from the local computer. Locally retained configuration and publishing records are deleted by the account owner. Published posts remain on Instagram unless the account owner deletes them there.

Privacy requests

To ask a question or request deletion of information held by this local integration, contact Denica Home through Instagram @homedenica. Do not send passwords or access tokens in a message.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the integration's data access or use changes.
